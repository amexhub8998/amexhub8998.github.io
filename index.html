Download Skill Recorder Now

Skill Recorder captures your manual work steps on your computer screen. You record a process one time, and the software creates a reusable workflow for your AI agent.

🎯 How it works

The software watches your screen while you perform a task. It tracks your mouse clicks, the apps you open, the websites you visit, and your spoken instructions. Once you finish the task, the app uses intelligent processing to turn those actions into a clear list of steps.

You can transform this list into a formal skill for your AI agent. The agent then performs the task for you on demand or based on a set schedule. It uses built-in tools to complete the work efficiently.

⚙️ System Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • 4 GB of system RAM
  • Active internet connection for cloud processing
  • Microphone (optional, for narration)

📥 Installation

Follow these steps to set up the software on your Windows machine:

  1. Visit the download page to get the latest version.
  2. Locate the Windows installer file in your downloads folder.
  3. Double-click the file to start the setup process.
  4. Follow the instructions on your screen to complete the installation.
  5. Launch the program from your desktop shortcut or start menu.

🚀 Your First Recording

Learning to use the system requires no coding knowledge. Open the application and look for the large record button. Click this button to start your capture.

Perform your task as you normally would. Switch between apps, click buttons, and enter text. If you want to log your thoughts, turn on the microphone and speak while you record. When you complete the task, press the stop button.

The software shows you a summary of the actions it detected. You can review these steps and save them as a new skill. The agent learns from this one example to handle similar tasks in the future without your help.

🛠️ Common Features

  • Automatic step detection: The app builds the logic based on your inputs.
  • Skill management: Store and organize your recorded processes in a library.
  • Flexible triggers: Choose between one-time execution or scheduled tasks.
  • Narration processing: Convert voice instructions into actionable computer steps.
